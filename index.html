Writing and formatting

How do I make a checklist?

Type [] at the start of a line, or tap the checklist button above the keyboard. Tap a box to tick it — any time, even while typing. Return adds the next item; Return on an empty item ends the list. The ⋯ menu can untick everything or remove ticked items.

What formatting is there?

Checklists, bullets (- ), numbered lists (1. ), headings (# ), quotes (> ), dividers (---), **bold**, *italic*, ~~strike~~ and `code`. Type the marks, or use the buttons above the keyboard. Notes are saved as plain text, so they read well wherever you share them.

How do I nest a list?

Use the indent and outdent buttons above the keyboard. Return keeps the same level.

Undo and redo

The arrows above the keyboard — or at the top of the note when you’re not typing — undo and redo typing, formatting, ticks and photos. For bigger steps back, use History.

Organising

Folders

Tap the folder button beside search to switch folders, or to make, rename and delete them. Move a note from its ⋯ menu. A new note goes into the folder you’re looking at. Deleting a folder never deletes its notes — they move to No folder.

Tags

Type a word with a hash anywhere in a note — #work, #recipes. Tags appear as chips above the list; tap one to show only those notes.

Photos

Tap the photo button above the keyboard, or Add photo in the ⋯ menu. Tap a photo in a note to remove it. Photos stay on your device and are never given to the AI.

Search

Search finds notes containing every word you type, in any order. Open a result and the matches are highlighted inside the note; tap × on the gold bar to clear them.

The AI

Does it need the internet?

No. The model is built into the app and runs on your device. It works in flight mode.

Why is the first AI action slow?

The model loads the first time you use an AI tool in a session — a few seconds on recent devices. After that, actions start straight away.

The AI got something wrong

It’s a small model and it can mix up names or details. Nothing changes until you tap Replace or Add; rewrites have a Changes view; added figures are flagged; answers in Ask show the matching sentence from your own note. Any applied change can be undone from the pop-up, the undo arrow, or History.

Why does a long note take several steps?

Long notes are worked through a few paragraphs at a time so the model never loses track — you’ll see “part 2 of 4”. Summaries of long notes are combined into one at the end.

Which languages can it translate to?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi and Thai.

History

When is a version saved?

Before every AI change, when you leave a note you edited, and whenever you tap Save version. Each note keeps its last five.

How do I go back?

Open the note, tap the clock icon, pick a version. “Changes since” shows what is different now, word by word; Restore puts it back. The text you restored over becomes the newest version, so you can switch back again.

Your notes

How do I back up my notes?

Settings → Export all notes as text, then save or send the file wherever you like. The app has no sync of its own.

The app said my notes were recovered

The saved file couldn’t be read, so the app restored the backup it keeps beside it. The last few seconds of typing may be missing. The damaged file is kept, not deleted.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

No account, no sign-in, no subscription.
